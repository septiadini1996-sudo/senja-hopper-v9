**ARCHIPEL AIR — Archipelago Air Mail.** A true-3D seaplane mail game in plain WebGL2 (hand-written engine, no libraries): fly hops across eight Indonesian islands — Bali → Nusa Penida → Lombok → Sumbawa → Flores → Sulawesi → Maluku → Papua — thread golden rings for bonus pay, and put the plane down on the destination airport's runway for a graded landing (S/A/B/C/D) and a TERKIRIM-stamped receipt. Every flight begins on land: she lines up on the home airport's paved runway — an island airfield with terminal, tower and hangars, ringed by real mountains — and the takeoff is a real ground roll; never rotate and the departure is aborted at the runway's end. She flies like a real aeroplane: lift lives in the airspeed, so cut the throttle and she comes down, and below the stall the nose drops and the sink runs away — power is how you stay up. A compressed 24-hour game clock drives the whole sky — dawn mist, harsh midday *terik*, the golden *senja* hour, and moonlit nights bright enough to fly by, lit by runway lamps, house windows and the plane's own landing light — and a deterministic (day, hour) weather table deals clear skies, overcast, rain and storms: every ticket on the morning board prints its departure hour and its forecast, and the flight keeps the board's promise. The board deals more than mail now: passenger runs graded on smoothness, sea rescues over a waiting boat, survey-photo gates, express runs against the clock, time-trial ring courses and storm pay — each with a live objective tracker on the HUD. A hangar with ENGINE / FUEL TANK / HULL / HANDLING upgrades and a fleet of four airframes to buy and switch between (payload class gates the heavy work), challenges, pilot stats, and a persistent save. Four control schemes — keyboard, joystick, touch, gyro — over one normalized input frame, with ASSISTED/DIRECT handling, three camera views (chase near, chase far, and a real cockpit: canopy frame and tinted glass, a live instrument panel and its own radar scope), and a free-look camera — drag the view, or I/J/K/L — that turns the pilot's head the full 360° while the plane holds her course. Full English / Bahasa Indonesia switching. Runs in the browser, touch-first on iPad.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts        # then open http://localhost:8080
```

Or open the hosted copy on this repository's GitHub Pages site. No build step, no dependencies: `game/` is static files and ES modules.
